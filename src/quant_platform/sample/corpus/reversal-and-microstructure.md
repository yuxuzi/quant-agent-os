# Short-term reversal and market microstructure

## Summary

Short-term reversal is the tendency of last month's losers to outperform last month's winners.
In US equities the one-month reversal effect is strongest among small, illiquid stocks.

## Mechanism

Part of the reversal effect is explained by bid-ask bounce rather than mispricing.
Short-term reversal correlates with liquidity provision returns, because market makers earn the
spread when they absorb temporary order flow.

## Costs

After transaction costs, decile sorts on one-month returns lose most of their Sharpe ratio.
Turnover for the reversal portfolio exceeds 400% per year.
