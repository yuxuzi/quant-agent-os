## Larchmere CLO 2018-2: Class E overcollateralization failure

The Class E overcollateralization test failed on the 31 March 2025 determination date. The CCC bucket stood
at 18.1% of performing par after a cluster of downgrades to Caa1 between December and February, and one
obligor defaulted in January. The excess over 7.5% was carried at market value. Interest after Class E
interest was diverted, so the distribution to the subordinated notes on the 21 April 2025 payment date is
zero. The test cured in the next report as upgrades came through.
