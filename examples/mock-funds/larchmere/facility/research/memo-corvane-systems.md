# Credit memo: Corvane Systems

Corvane Systems is a second-lien borrower in IT Consulting, held in Larchmere CLO 2021-1. The second-lien
bucket of that deal was 10.4% in January 2025 against a 12% limit, so we do not recommend adding.

## Thesis

The first-lien lenders are covered 1.6 times by enterprise value. Our recovery estimate for the second
lien is 35 to 45 points. The facility pays SOFR plus 760 basis points and prices near 94.

## Risks

Customer concentration: the top three customers are 41% of revenue. A default would be carried at market
value in the OC numerator, and the loan would leave the WARF and WAS calculations.
