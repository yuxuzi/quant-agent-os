# Indenture excerpt: coverage test definitions (Larchmere CLO 2021-1)

## Class A/B, C, D, and E overcollateralization ratio

The ratio on any date is the Adjusted Collateral Principal Amount divided by the aggregate outstanding
principal amount of the Class and all Classes senior to it. The Adjusted Collateral Principal Amount is the
aggregate principal balance of Collateral Obligations other than Defaulted Obligations and Excess CCC
Obligations, plus Principal Proceeds held as cash, plus for each Defaulted Obligation the lower of its
Market Value and its Recovery Amount, plus for each Excess CCC Obligation its Market Value.

## Excess CCC Obligations

Collateral Obligations rated Caa1 or lower by Moody's in an amount over 7.5% of the aggregate principal
balance of Collateral Obligations. The Excess CCC Obligations are the lowest priced.

## Interest coverage

The ratio of Interest Proceeds on the Collateral Obligations to interest due on the Class and all senior
Classes. When any overcollateralization test fails on a determination date, Interest Proceeds remaining after
interest on the Class E Notes are applied to the Class A Notes principal, and nothing is paid to the
Subordinated Notes.
