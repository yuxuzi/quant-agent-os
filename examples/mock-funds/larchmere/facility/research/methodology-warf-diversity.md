# Methodology summary: rating factors and the diversity score

The weighted average rating factor is the par-weighted average of each obligor's rating factor from the
agency's published table: Ba3 is 1766, B1 is 2220, B2 is 2720, B3 is 3490, Caa1 is 4770, Caa2 is 6500, and
a defaulted obligor is 10000. A lower WARF is a better portfolio. The diversity score counts obligors in
each industry as equivalent units, with an average obligor par amount, and maps units to a score by a
table that reaches 4.0 at ten units. Larchmere's own tool uses a simplified version and the trustee reports
the agency's own, which runs about two points higher.
