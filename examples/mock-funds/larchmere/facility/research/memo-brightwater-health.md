# Credit memo: Brightwater Health

Brightwater Health is a first-lien term loan B borrower in the Healthcare Providers industry. It is held in
Larchmere CLO 2018-2 and sized at about 1.1% of that pool. The facility is rated B3 with a stable outlook.

## Thesis

Same-store revenue grew 4% and leverage fell to 5.8 times EBITDA after the sponsor's equity cure. The loan
prices near 97 and pays SOFR plus 385 basis points with a 50 basis point floor. We recommend holding.

## Risks

A reimbursement change could cut EBITDA by 8%. A downgrade to Caa1 would put the loan in the CCC bucket
and, if the bucket is already above 7.5%, it would be carried at market value in the OC numerator.
