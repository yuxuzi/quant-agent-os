# Halden Retail Group: restructuring note

Halden Retail Group missed its coupon on the senior secured 2031 notes and filed for protection on 12 September 2025, the
date we record as the default. The distressed book held both the secured 2031 A notes and the senior unsecured 2032 B
notes.

## Recovery

The advisers' plan values the enterprise at 3.1 times EBITDA after closing 140 stores. We use a recovery of 58 percent
for the senior secured notes and 31 percent for the senior unsecured notes. The notes trade at those prices without
accrued interest from the default date.

## What we do

We keep the secured position and take the plan's equity-linked recovery rather than selling at 58. The unsecured position
is held for the settlement of the guarantee claims. Our jump-to-default estimate used the standard assumptions of 60 and 40
percent, so the secured notes recovered slightly less than assumed and the unsecured notes about nine points less.
