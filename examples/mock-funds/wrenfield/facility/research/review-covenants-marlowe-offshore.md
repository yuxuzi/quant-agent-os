# Covenant review: Marlowe Offshore

Marlowe Offshore is a fictional drilling contractor. The 2028 senior unsecured notes carry an incurrence test of 3.5x
fixed-charge coverage, a limit of 1.5x on secured debt to EBITDA, and a change of control put at 101.

## Findings

- Restricted payments are permitted while the incurrence test is met; the cushion is thin after the rig impairment.
- The secured-debt basket would let the company prime the notes by 200 million, which the market has not priced.
- Cross-default to the revolver triggers at 50 million, so a revolver default accelerates the notes.

We hold the notes in the high-yield book and recommend cutting the position if coverage drops below 2.5x.
