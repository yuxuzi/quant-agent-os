# Risk committee, October 2025

Present: the head of risk, the four portfolio managers, treasury, and compliance.

## Limits

- Fund DTS limit: 1,200 basis-point years on the sum of the books, measured on the three cash books' capital.
- Issuer limit: 7.5 percent of a book's capital gross; 5 percent for single-B and below.
- A scenario of three simultaneous defaults must not lose more than 4 percent of the fund's capital.

## Discussion

The committee reviewed the Halden default and agreed that jump-to-default estimates should use the recovery by seniority
(60, 40, and 15 percent) rather than the issuer's own plan. Marlowe Offshore is on the watch list after its second
downgrade. Treasury reported repo at SOFR plus 35 basis points on a 10 percent haircut, with specials on three shorts.

## Actions

Risk will report DTS by book and by rating bucket weekly. The overlay book will add protection on the high-yield index if
the fund's DTS exceeds 90 percent of the limit.
