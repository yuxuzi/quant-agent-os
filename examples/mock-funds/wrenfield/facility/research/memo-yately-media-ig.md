# Yately Media: credit memo

Yately Media is a fictional broadcaster rated in the BBB category. The investment-grade relative-value book holds its
2033 A, 2033 B, and 2034 B notes against five-year single-name CDS protection at a hedge ratio of 0.9.

## Why the trade works

The bonds trade cheap to the CDS: the basis is negative because the notes carry a liquidity premium after a large
index-eligible issue. We earn the carry between the bond's spread and the CDS premium, financed in repo at the book's
standard 90 percent advance, and close the position if the basis falls below 10 basis points negative.

## What can go wrong

A downgrade to high yield would force index sellers and widen the bond, but the CDS would widen too. A jump to default is
hedged to within the 10 percent unhedged part and the recovery assumption. Funding is the main risk: if the repo rate
rises 50 basis points the carry falls by about a third.
