# Relative value: negative basis

We propose buying the 2030 to 2035 senior notes of three issuers that trade 40 or more basis points wide of their
five-year CDS, with matching protection. The basis is the CDS spread at the bond's maturity, interpolated between three
and ten years, minus the bond's option-adjusted spread.

## Sizing

Each leg is 15 million with a hedge ratio of 0.9. The cost of carry is the repo rate plus 35 basis points on 90 percent of
the notional. The expected return is the negative basis closing over three months, about 30 basis points on the notional,
less a 10 basis point financing drag.

## Risks

A technical squeeze in the CDS can widen the basis. The trade needs the bond to stay investment grade.
