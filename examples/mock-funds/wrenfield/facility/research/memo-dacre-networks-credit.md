# Dacre Networks: credit memo

Dacre Networks is a fictional network-equipment maker rated BB-. We hold three senior unsecured notes in the high-yield
long/short book (the 2028, 2033 A, and 2033 B maturities), the largest single-issuer exposure in the book.

## Thesis

Leverage fell from 4.1x to 3.4x net debt to EBITDA over four quarters as the company repaid its revolver with free cash
flow. We expect a path to BB and spread compression of 40 to 60 basis points on the 2033 notes if leverage reaches 3.0x.

## Risks

- Customer concentration: the top two carriers are 46 percent of revenue.
- The 2033 notes are callable from 2030 at 102.5, so their spread duration is shorter than their maturity suggests.
- Sector spread beta to the high-yield index is about 1.2 in the last six months.

## Position and limits

The issuer limit is 7.5 percent of the book's capital gross, and we are close to it. No CDS is traded on the name, so the
position cannot be hedged except with the high-yield index overlay.
