# Marlow Event Opportunities: manager due-diligence memo

Marlow's estimates are conservative. Month after month the administrator's final return has come in above the
manager's estimate, by 14 basis points a month on average over the last 58 months. We treat the estimate as a
floor and carry the position at the estimate until the final arrives, then book the difference. Operations
reconciles the difference each month-end.

Final NAVs arrive about 45 days after month-end, so the two most recent months are always estimates. The team
is comfortable with the strategy and asks the risk team to measure how much of the reported Sharpe ratio comes
from month-end marks.
