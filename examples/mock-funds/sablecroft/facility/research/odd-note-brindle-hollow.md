# Operational due diligence: Brindle Hollow Activist

The administrator is independent and the auditor is a recognised firm. Findings: valuation of two illiquid
positions is done by the manager and not by the administrator; side-pocket policy exists but has never been
used; key-person dependency on the founder is high.

Rating: acceptable with conditions. The conditions were a quarterly valuation attestation and a lower
allocation limit of 3%. The manager announced a wind-down in June 2024 and the fund's last NAV was struck
on 2024-06-30.
