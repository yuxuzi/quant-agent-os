# Harbourlight Equity Partners: manager due-diligence memo

Harbourlight runs a concentrated long/short book of about 40 names with net exposure between 30% and 50%.
Monthly NAV estimates arrive five days after month-end and the administrator's final about fifteen days
after. Over the last 36 months the manager's net exposure to the market was around 0.45 and alpha was positive
but not statistically different from zero at a 5% level.

Terms: 12-month rolling lock-up, 45 days' notice, quarterly dealing, with an investor-level gate of 25% of the
position per dealing date. The team recommends holding the allocation at 4% of the fund.
