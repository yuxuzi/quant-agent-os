# Stock pitch: Lorenton Corp (US0023), long, Industrials

Author: the Industrials analyst. Date: 2026-01-21. Strategy: US Large Cap Core.

Lorenton supplies precision parts to the same customers as Coromere, with less exposure to new
equipment and more to maintenance. We see it as the defensive half of our machinery view.

The stock trades at a discount to its own five-year average on enterprise value to cash flow. Our
quality score for it is in the top decile of the vendor's universe. We propose twice the
benchmark weight.

Risks: a customer concentration of 22% of sales, and a pension deficit that would widen if rates
fall. Neither is a reason to stay out at this size.
