# Factor research note: the vendor's quality score

Author: the quant researcher. Date: 2026-05-20.

We tested the four vendor factors (quality, value, momentum, low volatility) as cross-sectional
signals for next-day returns over the first five months of 2026, about 400 securities.

**Quality** showed a positive mean rank information coefficient of about 0.04, and the sign was
stable across months. **Value**, **momentum** and **low volatility** were indistinguishable from
zero over this short window. A window this short supports no claim about their long-run value.

The vendor delivers scores monthly, two business days into the month. We carry each score forward
until the next delivery and use it from the delivery date. A score is therefore up to a month
old, which makes a one-day horizon the fairest test and a 21-day horizon an optimistic one,
because the same score is paired with overlapping returns.

Before we trade a quality tilt we want a test with costs and a sector-neutral construction, which
our research tools do not provide yet.
