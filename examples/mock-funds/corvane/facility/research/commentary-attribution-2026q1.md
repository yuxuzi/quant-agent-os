# Quarterly attribution commentary: first quarter 2026

Author: client service with the portfolio managers. For clients of US Large Cap Core.

This commentary explains where the strategy's active return came from.
We attribute by Brinson-Fachler on the eleven sectors, with cash as its own line, and link the
monthly results by the Carino method so that the pieces add to the quarter's active return.

- **Selection** was a main contributor. Industrials selection was the largest of any sector,
  led by our machinery holdings. Information Technology and Health Care also added.
- **Allocation**: the overweight in Industrials added, because the sector beat the benchmark.
- **Cash** held about 1.5% of the portfolio and cost a little in a rising market.

Our attribution is computed from end-of-day holdings, and a position bought during a month
appears from the next month's start.

This commentary is for clients of the strategy. It does not give the holdings of any other
client's account.
