# Investment committee minutes, 9 April 2026

Present: the two portfolio managers, the head of risk, the head of research, compliance.

1. **Industrials.** The committee reviewed the machinery position. It agreed to hold the
   overweight in Industrials through the second quarter and to trim any single name that exceeds
   5% of US Large Cap Core. Risk will report the trimming candidates weekly.
2. **Corporate action: Jorargate spin-off.** The spin-off of the tobacco business on 12 March put
   shares of a tobacco company into every account that held the parent. The Brightwater Foundation
   and Cantor Sovereign Fund mandates exclude tobacco. The shares were sold on 18 March in all
   accounts. Compliance will add a pre-trade check for spin-offs. The committee noted four trading
   days in which two restricted accounts held an excluded name.
3. **Delisting.** Alurridge Works was acquired for cash and delisted on 22 April (expected). Proceeds
   will be reinvested at the May rebalance.
4. **Currency.** Global Quality remains unhedged. The committee asked for the currency effect to
   be shown separately in the monthly attribution.

Actions: risk to report trimming candidates; compliance to add the spin-off check.
