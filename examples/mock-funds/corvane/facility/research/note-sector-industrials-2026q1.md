# Sector note: Industrials, first quarter 2026

Author: the Industrials analyst. Date: 2026-04-02.

We were overweight Industrials, and the sector beat the benchmark, so the overweight added value
through allocation. Our four machinery holdings returned more than the sector, so stock selection
in Industrials was the largest selection effect in US Large Cap Core.

Our reading is that machinery orders held up while transport, where we hold little, lagged. We do not expect that gap to persist at this size; see the attribution
commentary for the figures and the investment committee minute for the decision.

Data note: the vendor's industry labels put two of our names in Machinery that we would call
Aerospace. We have not changed the labels.
