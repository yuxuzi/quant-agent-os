# Stock pitch: Coromere Holdings (US0018), long, Industrials

Author: the Industrials analyst. Date: 2026-01-14. Strategy: US Large Cap Core.

Coromere makes automated assembly machinery for mid-sized manufacturers. Orders have grown faster
than the sector for three quarters, and the backlog now covers about nine months of revenue.

**Why we own it.** Margins are rising because service contracts are a growing share of sales. We
see the market pricing in a cyclical peak, while the backlog says otherwise.

**Sizing.** We propose 2.5 times the benchmark weight, which keeps it below the 6% single-name
limit of the strategy. A shortfall in orders next quarter is the main risk, and we would cut the
position by half on a book-to-bill below 1.0.

**Catalysts.** The April investor day, and the second-quarter order release.
