# Carry signal: review for 2023-2024

## Result

Across the 20 non-equity instruments (bond futures, G10 FX, credit indices, commodities), the carry
signal's mean daily rank IC is indistinguishable from zero: the point estimate is slightly negative and
its t-statistic is well inside plus or minus two.

## Interpretation

Carry pays when spot is stable; our sample had several reversals in funding currencies. A zero IC over
two years does not prove carry is dead, but it is not evidence that the desk's carry tilt adds value.

## Recommendation

Hold the carry weight in the macro desk's blend at 40% until the risk committee's next review, but do
not raise it. Re-test with a longer history and with transaction costs included.
