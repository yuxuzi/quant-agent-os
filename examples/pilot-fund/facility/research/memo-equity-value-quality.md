# Strategy memo: equity value and quality

## Thesis

The value premium, buying cheap stocks by book-to-market and selling expensive ones, has been weak in
the US since 2010 but remains positive in Europe and when combined with quality. Quality minus junk
(QMJ) buys profitable, stable, well-governed firms. The two are negatively correlated, so the blend
has a better Sharpe ratio than either alone.

## Universe and signal

Twenty names: twelve US and eight European, across technology, health care, financials, industrials,
and consumer. Value is book-to-price z-scored within region, so a cheap European bank is compared with
other European names, not with US software.

## Portfolio

The desk blends 70% momentum and 30% value, market-neutral by construction, gross exposure 100% of
capital. Rebalance weekly.

## Risks

Value traps, and sector concentration: a value book leans toward financials and energy. The desk checks
its factor exposures against the vendor model each month.
