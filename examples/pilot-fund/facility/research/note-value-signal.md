# Equity value signal

Book-to-price on the 20-name equity universe has a mean daily rank IC of about 0.018. It is small and
noisy, with a t-statistic near two, consistent with the weak value premium in recent years.

Grouping matters: z-scoring within region rather than across the whole universe raises the IC, because
European names are structurally cheaper than US names on book-to-market. We recommend keeping value at
30% of the equity desk's blend, where it diversifies momentum, and reviewing it with portfolio sorts
once a year.
