# Short-term reversal: not adopted

One-month reversal, buying last month's losers and selling its winners, shows a positive gross IC on
the US equities in our universe but does not survive transaction costs: it needs daily rebalancing, and
part of its apparent return is bid-ask bounce in the closing prices. With the desk's cost model, the net
Sharpe ratio is negative. We do not recommend it, and we record the result so it is not re-tested
without new data.
