# Risk committee minutes, 26 September 2025

## Limit usage

No breaches this quarter. One warning: the macro desk reached 85% of its VaR limit in August during
the dollar move, and cut FX gross by a fifth.

## Decisions

- The macro desk's dollar concentration is accepted as intended, with a sub-limit: the US dollar
  rally scenario loss must stay under USD 35m.
- Limits are unchanged otherwise. The equity desk asked for a higher gross limit; declined until its
  value signal shows a stronger IC.
- From the fourth quarter, risk reports are produced through the new research platform, and every
  figure must cite the dataset it was computed from.
