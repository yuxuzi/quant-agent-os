# Momentum crowding and crash risk

Cross-sectional momentum crashes after sharp market rebounds, when the short leg, last year's losers,
rallies hardest. Factor crowding makes the crash deeper: when many managers hold the same winners and
losers, they sell together.

We monitor crowding on the equity desk by the pairwise correlation of its longs and its shorts. When
that correlation is in the top decile of its history, the desk halves the momentum weight in its blend
for a month. Over 2023-2025 this rule triggered twice and cost little in expected return. The maximum
drawdown of the momentum sleeve fell by about a quarter in the backtest.
