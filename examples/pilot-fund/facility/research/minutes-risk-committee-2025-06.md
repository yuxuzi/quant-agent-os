# Risk committee minutes, 27 June 2025

## Stress scenarios approved

Five scenarios, applied to factor loadings from the vendor model: equity crash (2008 style), rates
shock +100bp, US dollar rally, oil supply shock, and credit event. Each desk's loss in its worst
scenario is checked against its worst-scenario loss limit every day.

## Findings

- The macro desk's model risk is about 90% US dollar factor. The committee asked the PM to show that
  this is intended; FX carry and the dollar rally scenario move together.
- The commodities desk's worst scenario is the oil supply shock when it is net short crude.

## Actions

- Macro PM to present the dollar concentration in September.
- Research to re-test carry with costs before any increase in its weight.
