# Reading factor exposures

Factor exposures come from a regression of portfolio returns on factor returns. An exposure shows
how much of a factor a book holds on average over the sample, not what it holds today.

A high r-squared means the factors explain most of the book's variance. The specific share is what
they leave unexplained, and it is the part that diversification across names can reduce.
