# Momentum crash risk in concentrated growth books

Cross-sectional momentum is measured with 12-1 momentum, skipping the most recent month to avoid
short-term reversal.

Momentum contradicts the reversal effect at the one-month horizon. Momentum crashes follow market rebounds. In a rebound month, the recent losers rise fastest, so a
book that holds the recent winners can lose money while the market gains.

Concentrated growth portfolios often carry a positive momentum exposure without having chosen it.
We report the maximum drawdown and the Sharpe ratio of such portfolios, and we recommend reviewing
the momentum exposure whenever the market has fallen for two consecutive months.
