# Value and quality in a long-only book

The value factor buys cheap stocks, measured here by the book-to-market ratio. Value tends to
offset momentum: the two factors have been negatively correlated over long samples.

Combining value with the quality factor reduces the maximum drawdown of a long-only book. Turnover
is low, so transaction costs matter less than they do for momentum.
